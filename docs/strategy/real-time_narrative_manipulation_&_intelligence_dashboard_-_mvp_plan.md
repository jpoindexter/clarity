# Real-Time Narrative Manipulation & Intelligence Dashboard – MVP Plan


## Overview

The Real-Time Narrative Manipulation & Intelligence Dashboard is a core component of Clarity's intelligence platform. It detects, analyzes, and visualizes manipulation tactics across multiple domains, including financial markets, geopolitical events, institutional data, and news media.


## Purpose

Identify manipulation in real-time across multiple data sources.

Surface contradictions and inconsistencies in narratives.

Analyze financial & geopolitical disinformation and its impact.

Support intelligence-driven decision-making for institutional users.


## Key Features


### 1. Multi-Source Data Aggregation 

Ingests real-time data from financial feeds, government records, media reports, and intelligence sources.

Tracks AI-generated disinformation and coordinated influence campaigns.

Captures market-moving narratives and misinformation.


### 2. Narrative Tracking & Contradiction Detection

AI models detect inconsistencies in public statements, market reports, and news stories.

Flags entities involved in spreading conflicting narratives.

Predicts potential financial & geopolitical impact.


### 3. Real-Time Market & Sentiment Analysis

Identifies manipulative news cycles affecting stocks, crypto, and commodities.

Maps investor sentiment shifts driven by narrative changes.

Detects coordinated media disinformation affecting financial trends.


### 4. Misinformation & AI-Generated Content Detection

Flags AI-generated reports, deepfakes, and fabricated market reports.

Links back to originating sources to assess credibility.

Uses historical accuracy scoring to evaluate trustworthiness.


### 5. Entity Mapping & Network Analysis

Visualizes connections between sources, media outlets, political actors, and financial influencers.

Identifies high-risk entities that spread disinformation.

Predicts impact on financial markets, elections, and regulatory actions.


## How It Works


### 1. Data Pipeline Ingestion 

Structured/unstructured data feeds processed in real-time.


### 2. AI & NLP Analysis 

Detection models analyze for bias, contradictions, and manipulation patterns.


### 3. Visualization & Alerts 

Interactive dashboard highlights anomalies and sends alerts.


### 4. Impact Prediction 

Models estimate the potential influence on financial, geopolitical, or media landscapes.


## Target Users

Institutional Investors & Hedge Funds → Real-time detection of market-moving misinformation.

Government & Regulatory Agencies → Tracking foreign influence, misinformation, and manipulation tactics.

Journalists & Media Analysts → Investigating coordinated propaganda & false narratives.

Think Tanks & Intelligence Firms → High-level strategic insights for global decision-making.
