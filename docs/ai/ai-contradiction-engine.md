# Clarity AI – Contradiction Detection & Intelligence Engine

## Overview

Clarity's AI intelligence processing framework is designed to analyze global information streams, detect patterns, surface contradictions, and provide real-time decision intelligence. This document outlines the key AI-powered capabilities that drive Clarity's intelligence engine.

## Contradiction Detection

### Objective
Identify contradictory statements across news, financial reports, policy updates, and geopolitical events.

### Methodology
- Compare statements from different sources using NLP-based semantic analysis.
- Track changes over time (e.g., political figures contradicting previous statements).
- Flag inconsistencies in financial forecasts vs. actual earnings data. (e.g., Mistral 7B, Claude 3, or local LLMs via Ollama)

### Output
Highlighted contradictions with contextual metadata (source, entity, timestamp, confidence score).

## Event Correlation & Causal Analysis

### Objective
Link real-world events to financial, political, and social outcomes.

### Methodology
- Utilize time-series AI models to correlate news, economic shifts, and social sentiment with market movements.
- Identify early warning signals (e.g., policy changes affecting stock performance).
- Leverage graph-based AI to detect underlying patterns.

### Output
Predictive insights on cascading effects of major events.

## Sentiment & Bias Analysis

### Objective
Measure sentiment polarity and detect potential media biases in news and opinion pieces.

### Methodology
- NLP-driven sentiment scoring across various sources.
- Detect ideological leaning and bias using a reference dataset of known biases.
- Compare coverage discrepancies across media outlets.

### Output
Sentiment trends, bias heatmaps, and trustworthiness ratings.

## Credibility Scoring & Source Validation

### Objective
Assess the reliability of sources and flag potential misinformation.

### Methodology
- Utilize reputation analysis based on historical accuracy of sources.
- Cross-check claims against trusted knowledge bases and independent fact-checkers.
- Detect synthetic or AI-generated misinformation patterns.

### Output
Credibility scores with supporting evidence.

## Geopolitical Risk Assessment

### Objective
Identify emerging geopolitical risks and their potential global impact.

### Methodology
- AI-driven entity recognition to track political, military, and economic shifts.
- Predictive modeling for economic sanctions, conflicts, and trade disruptions.
- Real-time mapping of geopolitical events and their interconnections.

### Output
Risk scores, heatmaps, and scenario modeling.

## Market Impact Forecasting

### Objective
Predict financial market responses to real-world events and news narratives.

### Methodology
- AI-powered trend analysis on economic indicators, earnings reports, and social sentiment.
- Deep learning models for asset price movement prediction based on narrative shifts.
- Signal extraction for high-impact market-moving news. Clarity supports offline model inference via Ollama (Mistral, DeepSeek, LLaMA), ensuring data privacy and local reasoning when needed.

### Output
Market trend forecasts with confidence intervals.

## Integration with Clarity Intelligence Engine

All AI-driven insights feed into the Clarity Intelligence Platform, allowing institutional clients, financial analysts, and decision-makers to interact with real-time intelligence layers.

- **API Integration:** AI insights are accessible via API endpoints.
- **Graph Visualization:** Relationship mapping between events, entities, and financial impact.
- **Automated Alerts:** Custom triggers for anomaly detection and critical intelligence updates.
